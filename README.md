# 📌 Project Overview

The **Hospital Management Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze hospital operations, patient care, financial performance, and clinical analytics.

The dashboard enables healthcare administrators to monitor KPIs, analyze patient trends, evaluate treatment costs, explore hospital records, and gain actionable business insights for data-driven decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor hospital performance through KPI dashboards
- Analyze patient demographics and clinical trends
- Track revenue, treatment costs, and medication expenses
- Explore detailed patient records
- Generate actionable business insights
- Improve operational efficiency using interactive visualizations

# 📌 Business Insights

✔ Patient satisfaction remained consistently around **4/5**.

✔ Follow-up visits indicate opportunities for improving patient engagement.

✔ Treatment costs remained stable across major diagnosis categories.

✔ Business recommendations were developed to improve operational efficiency.
